## How do you prepare to fish in Clover Time?

Prepare a **fishing rod** and **bait**, then travel to river or sea water. Fishing supplies are part of the village merchant and equipment systems. Equip the rod from your inventory before starting the fishing interaction, and leave room to inspect your catches so that you can decide what to keep after the session.

## What is the PC sequence for catching a fish?

With the rod equipped near river or sea water, press **V** to begin the fishing interaction and select bait. Wait for a bite, complete the clicking minigame, and inspect your inventory for the catch. Follow the active catch interaction through its stages before opening another menu or starting a new attempt.

1. Equip the fishing rod.
2. Approach river or sea water.
3. Press V and choose bait.
4. Wait for the bite interaction.
5. Complete the clicking minigame.
6. Check the catch in your inventory.

These are PC action labels. Match the fishing action to the controls on your own device.

## How should you organise fish and Mana Crystal materials?

Keep **fish**, **Mana Crystal Fragments**, and complete **Mana Crystals** as separate inventory categories. Golden Marlin belongs to the fishing and magic-material progression path, while whole crystals are the resource used for Grimoire rolls. Inspect the item name before planning the next action so that a material is not mistaken for a completed crystal.

| Inventory category | Place in progression | Useful distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rod and bait | Fishing equipment | Prepare before the catch interaction. |
| Fish | Catches to keep or sell | Decide what to retain before selling. |
| Mana Crystal Fragments | Magic materials | Distinct from whole crystals. |
| Mana Crystals | Grimoire roll resource | Used at the tower. |
| Spins | Character roll resource | Separate from fishing and Grimoire resources. |

Use the [Grimoire comparison](/guides/grimoire-tier-list/) when planning a magic-book choice. The [character-roll guide](/guides/how-to-reroll/) explains the separate Spins system.

## What should you consider before selling a fishing catch?

Fish-dealer NPCs buy catches for game currency, so review the items you want to keep before selling from your inventory. Track fishing catches separately from other rewards, including **AFK World** activity. This makes it easier to distinguish the equipment you use, the fish you caught, and the magic materials you are collecting.

<Callout type="tip">Read an item's name and role before selling it. A catch, a fragment, and a complete crystal occupy different places in progression.</Callout>
