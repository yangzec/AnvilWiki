## Which race should you compare first in Clover Time?

Compare **Devil** first for its broad passive package and **Elf** for mana-focused play. **Human-Dwarf** combines sustain and progression attributes, **Dwarf** emphasises sustain and resource capacity, and **Human** provides baseline regeneration and EXP benefits. This PvE ordering prioritises passive roles; your Grimoire and the abilities you use remain part of the choice.

The ordering compares passive packages, rather than a timed levelling trial or a damage leaderboard. It is useful for deciding what to inspect in the character panel.

## Which attributes distinguish the five race packages?

Look at the kinds of attributes each race supports: health, stamina, mana, regeneration, and EXP. **Elf** concentrates the comparison on mana capacity and recovery; **Devil** spans a broader package. **Human-Dwarf** and **Dwarf** offer sustain-oriented alternatives, while **Human** gives a starting point for comparing regeneration and progression benefits across the other choices.

| Race package | Attribute areas | Question for your build |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Human | Health, stamina, and mana regeneration; EXP | Which baseline benefit helps your current activity? |
| Dwarf | Regeneration, stamina, and mana | Do you want sustain and resource capacity? |
| Human-Dwarf | Regeneration, stamina, mana, and EXP | Do you want a mixture of sustain and progression? |
| Elf | Mana capacity, mana recovery, regeneration, and EXP | Is magic resource management your priority? |
| Devil | Health, regeneration, mana, and EXP | Does a broad passive package fit your character? |

Compare those roles with the [Grimoire tier list](/guides/grimoire-tier-list/). The book determines your magic tools, while race passives support the character using them.

## Why are Sovereign and Chosen One outside the race table?

**Sovereign** belongs to **Social Class**, and **Chosen One** belongs to **Trait**. They appear beside Race in the character configuration menu rather than inside the five-race list. A setup can combine entries from all three categories, so read each column independently when comparing a build or deciding which category you want to change.

The [reroll menu guide](/guides/how-to-reroll/) shows the distinction between Race, Social Class, and Trait. Mana Sensitive is also a Trait, rather than a sixth race.

## How can you apply the ranking before choosing another roll?

Read your current race and its displayed attributes, identify the benefit your play style needs, and compare that benefit with the table. Then check your Social Class and Trait alongside it. This makes the decision specific to your character rather than treating the highest tier as a reason to spend every available Spin.

For sword-oriented play, consider the [Anti Magic kit](/items/anti-magic/). Spell-oriented play has different positioning tools, as the [Poison guide](/items/poison/) shows. Learn those tools before judging how much a different passive package would help.
