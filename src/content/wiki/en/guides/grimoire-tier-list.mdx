## Which Grimoires lead this Clover Time PvE tier list?

**Anti Magic** and **Poison** lead this PvE comparison: one centres on sword pressure, movement, and a counter, while the other combines projectiles, continuing area damage, and a clone. **Ice**, **Explosive Flame**, and **Battle Lightning** form the next group. Treat these tiers as editorial priorities for comparing kits and choosing what to practise.

## How does acquisition rarity differ from a PvE tier?

Rarity groups books within the acquisition system, while the tier table compares how their kits fit PvE encounters. **Ice** is Uncommon but sits in the strong-alternative group; **Anti Magic** is Mythic and offers a sword kit. Use both pieces of information instead of reading rarity as a direct measure of combat performance.

| Tower rarity | Books in that group |
| --- | --- |
| Common | Earth, Fire, Snow, Water |
| Uncommon | Ice |
| Rare | Chain |
| Epic | Explosive Flame, Poison |
| Legendary | Battle Lightning |
| Mythic | Anti Magic |

Fire and Explosive Flame are separate entries. The [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) explains the tower marker and the Mana Crystal resource used for Grimoire rolls.

## Why choose Anti Magic or Poison for different encounters?

Choose **Anti Magic** for a sword-focused comparison involving close-range attacks, approach moves, and responses to pressure. Compare **Poison** when you want projectiles, damaging mist, an explosion, and clone control. The important decision is how those tools fit your positioning and targets, including whether an encounter involves one enemy or several.

## When should you keep developing your current Grimoire?

Keep practising a useful book while you learn its available moves, reach, and interaction with basic attacks. A different tier becomes relevant when you can explain which tool your current encounters need. Compare base abilities separately from transformations, and consider your character's mana and regeneration alongside the magic kit you already use.

Use [race passives](/guides/race-tier-list/) to understand the character side of that decision. Character rerolls change a separate system, so spending Spins does not roll another Grimoire.
