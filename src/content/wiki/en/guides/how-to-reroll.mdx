## How do you open Clover Time character rerolls?

Choose **Customize → Rolls** from the lobby, or open the character options through **Hage Priest** in the starting village's church. The panel places **Race**, **Social Class**, and **Trait** in separate columns. Read the selected result in each column and the balances at the top before choosing a Roll button.

![Fantasy illustration of a Clover Time character configuration altar](/images/articles/clover-time/reroll-concept.webp)

*Original concept illustration.*

The displayed heading separates **Spins** from **Special**. The button you select belongs to a specific category, so locate its column before interacting.

## What is the difference between Race, Social Class, and Trait?

**Race** identifies a passive attribute package, **Social Class** is a separate character status, and **Trait** is another passive option. A character can have one selection shown in each column. Names from the three lists therefore describe different parts of a setup rather than alternative names for the same system.

| Character column | Examples in that category | Comparison to make |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Race | Human, Dwarf, Human-Dwarf, Elf, Devil | Regeneration, capacity, and progression attributes |
| Social Class | Commoner, Sovereign | The status shown beside your race |
| Trait | Fast Learner, Chosen One, Mana Sensitive | The passive shown beside your class |

The [race tier list](/guides/race-tier-list/) compares all five races. Sovereign belongs in Social Class, and Chosen One belongs in Trait.

## How should you inspect a character-roll action?

Write down your current selections, choose the category you want to change, and read the balance and any prompt associated with its **Roll** button. Review the selected result after the action before making another choice. This keeps each decision tied to one category and the resource shown for that action.

1. Read the existing Race, Social Class, and Trait.
2. Identify the one category you want to change.
3. Inspect its button and associated resource prompt.
4. Review the selected result before proceeding.

<Callout type="tip">Decide which passive role supports your current play style before opening a roll menu. A preferred name by itself gives you less information than its attribute package.</Callout>

## Why are character Spins separate from Grimoire rolls?

Character rolls use the **Spins** system in the three-column configuration panel. Grimoire rolls use **Mana Crystals** at the **Grimoire Tower**, which changes your magic book rather than your Race, Social Class, or Trait. Choose the destination according to the part of your character that you actually want to develop.

The [Grimoire comparison](/guides/grimoire-tier-list/) covers magic-book roles. If you need to identify the tower marker and other opening systems, follow the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/).
