## How should a beginner start Clover Time?

Begin by reading your character options, checking the inventory and quest prompts, and practising **M1** basic attacks. Learn what **Race**, **Social Class**, and **Trait** each represent before spending resources. Then use the compass to identify destinations and explore the tool, Grimoire, and broom systems as their interactions become available.

## What should you look for in early gathering tasks?

Read village quest objectives alongside the tools in your inventory: an **Axe** chops trees, while a **Rake** is used for crop activities. Lily's potato task introduces crop gathering, and village wood tasks introduce collecting wood. Follow the requested activity and inspect the task before handing over the items you gather.

- Match the tool to the activity rather than treating all tools as weapons.
- Keep the objective visible while gathering.
- Check gathered items in the inventory before returning to the quest giver.

Fishing adds another equipment-based activity later in your exploration; the [fishing guide](/guides/fishing-fragments/) covers rods, bait, and the PC interaction.

## How can you learn combat before combining spells?

Practise **M1** attacks with the weapon you have, observe the enemy's reach, and move out of its next attack before committing again. Compare swords, daggers, or scythes through their reach and attack rhythm. Once you have magic, learn individual moves before trying to combine several attacks into one sequence.

1. Approach a readable PvE target.
2. Observe how close it must be to threaten you.
3. Use basic attacks and reposition between exchanges.
4. Add one spell at a time so its role stays clear.

This is a practice order, rather than a claim that every weapon shares the same timing or range.

## How do you locate the Grimoire Tower and its roll point?

Follow the **tower icon on the compass** to the Grimoire Tower and look for the **Grimoire Roll** interaction. The PC prompt includes **E — Read** and a crystal icon. Read the displayed requirement before selecting the interaction, keeping **Mana Crystals** separate from the Spins shown in your character configuration menu.

The [Grimoire tier list](/guides/grimoire-tier-list/) compares books by PvE role and rarity. Decide which kind of kit you want to learn before spending resources on another roll.

<Callout type="tip">Character choices and magic books use different menus and balances. Checking the resource label is useful before either kind of roll.</Callout>

## How does broom travel expand your exploration?

**Magic Brooms** provide flying movement, and **Master Elsie's Learning to Fly** quest introduces broom balance training. The PC interface uses **H — Spawn Broom** for broom access. As you progress through the broom system, practise travel between destinations separately from combat so that you can follow markers and recognise landing locations.

Magic Squads provide a way to team up for boss fights. The [Beta feature overview](/guides/patch-notes/) explains how flight, squads, magic progression, and AFK World fit into the wider game.
