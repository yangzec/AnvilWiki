Damage type is how Lootbound talks to enemy armor. A party that only speaks one language can hit a wall. The four names below come from a beginner tips article; Steam does not list them, so coefficients stay unmarked.

## What damage types does Lootbound use?

Four. Base, Slash, Crush, and Acid. Each meets armor differently. Official UI labels, icons, and whether more types exist are [待补充].

## How does each type interact with armor?

Armor is the cheap resource: it fully restores after battle. Health does not come back on its own. Use Crush or Acid when a fight is an armor wall; use Slash when the wall is already down; use Base when you need a safe default.

## Should I specialize in one type?

Not on a first run. Tips coverage says enemies are built to wall parties that only speak one language, and that you should keep at least one armor-breaker and one armor-ignorer in the group. Spreading four types thinly is also a trap — hold two languages, not one and not all four at 1%.

| Party plan | Works when | Fails when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One type only | [待补充] | You meet the wall for that type |
| Breaker + ignorer | You can still cluster auras | You scatter both across empty cells |
| All four at once | [待补充] | You never finish a district |

Warrior is reported to lean Slash. Witch is reported to lean Acid. Confirm in-game before you lock a wiki stat block.
