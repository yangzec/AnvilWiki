## What is Clover Time Beta 0.1?

**Clover Time** is an anime RPG developed by **AnimeCore Studio**, with its Roblox description identifying **Beta 0.1**. The game combines Grimoire progression, action combat, spell combinations, and exploration. Its wider feature set includes Awakenings, Magic Brooms, Magic Squads, and AFK World, giving players several connected systems to explore as their character develops.

## Which features shape the game's progression?

Grimoires provide magic-book progression, combat combines attacks and spells, and Awakenings add transformed magic kits. **Magic Brooms** support flying exploration, **Magic Squads** bring players together for boss fights, and **AFK World** provides another reward activity. ## Where can you follow updates on your platform?

Use the official Roblox experience, the **AnimeCore Studio** group, and the community server to follow game information and update previews. Clover Time supports **PC**, **mobile**, and **console**, so match action names to the controls shown on your device. After a change, review the affected system before spending resources to alter your setup.
