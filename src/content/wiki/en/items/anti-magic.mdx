## What is the Anti Magic Grimoire in Clover Time?

**Anti Magic** is a **Mythic** Grimoire built around sword combat, with attacks, movement tools, a counter, and an Awakening transformation. Its role centres on close-range exchanges and approaching targets. Compare the base kit first, then treat transformation practice as another part of progression rather than assuming every move behaves like a basic sword attack.

The Grimoire Tower handles book rolls using Mana Crystals. [Beginner tower navigation](/guides/beginner-guide/) explains the compass marker and interaction labels. Character Spins belong to a separate system.

## Which base sword skills and PC labels should you learn?

Learn **Anti Magic Cut**, **Counter**, **Dual Spin**, and **Rush Divider** as distinct tools: an attack, a response, a spinning strike, and an approach sequence. The PC labels pair them with **E**, **X**, **Z**, and **R**. Match those labels to your interface before practising each move alongside basic attacks.

| Sword tool | PC label | Role to practise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Anti Magic Cut | E | Judge the slash's reach and impact. |
| Counter | X | Learn a response to nearby pressure. |
| Dual Spin | Z | Compare the spinning attack with a basic exchange. |
| Rush Divider | R | Observe the approach and where the attack leaves you. |

On other devices, match the action name to the control shown on your own screen.

## How should you practise Anti Magic against PvE enemies?

Start with basic attacks and **Anti Magic Cut** to learn reach, then practise moving away from the enemy's next attack. Add **Counter** against pressure you can recognise, and use **Rush Divider** to examine approach distance and finishing position. Keeping these exercises separate helps you understand each tool before joining them into a longer exchange.

1. Observe an enemy's attack range and rhythm.
2. Compare a basic attack with Anti Magic Cut.
3. Practise a response and repositioning.
4. Add an approach tool once you understand where it leaves you.

<Callout type="tip">Judge a movement attack by both its entry and its finishing position. Getting close to a target is only one part of the exchange.</Callout>

## How do Awakening and Poison change the comparison?

Anti Magic's **Awakening** adds a transformation and a changed combat kit, so compare base sword tools and transformed abilities separately. **Poison** offers a different alternative through projectiles, damaging mist, an explosion, and a clone. Choose the comparison around your encounter and preferred positioning, rather than treating both books as interchangeable high-tier results.
