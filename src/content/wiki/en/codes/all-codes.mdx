## Where can you redeem Clover Time codes?

Use the **Codes** field on the lobby screen or the Codes section under **Menu → Settings** during play. Enter the promotion text exactly and submit it with **Enter**. The lobby and Settings are separate entry points, so choose the one that matches your current screen.

![Fantasy illustration of a Clover Time code reward chest](/images/articles/clover-time/codes-concept.webp)

*Original concept illustration.*

The lobby input uses **Enter code...** as its placeholder. For the menu-by-menu sequence, follow [how to redeem codes](/guides/how-to-redeem-codes/).

## How do you keep code text separate from an announcement?

Copy the promotion string itself, leaving out the announcement heading, reward description, and surrounding punctuation. Preserve characters that belong to the code, and remove spaces added before or after it. Submit one entry at a time and read the game's response before moving on to another promotion.

- Keep letters, numbers, and punctuation in the same order.
- Avoid copying a line break into the input.
- Record attempted codes so that you can recognise a repeated entry.
- Use the response to that attempt when deciding whether to continue.

## Which roll balance should you check after redemption?

Check the balance for the system you plan to use: **Spins** belong to character rolls, while **Mana Crystals** belong to Grimoire rolls. The character panel also displays a separate **Special** balance. These labels refer to different resources, so a change in one balance does not describe the others.

| Balance label | Relevant menu | Player decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spins | Race, Social Class, and Trait columns | Choose the character category you want to change. |
| Special | Separate balance in the character-roll panel | Read the associated option before selecting it. |
| Mana Crystals | Grimoire Tower rolling point | Compare books before using a crystal. |

Open [character rerolls](/guides/how-to-reroll/) for the three character categories. The [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) explains how character choices and Grimoire progression fit into the opening game.
