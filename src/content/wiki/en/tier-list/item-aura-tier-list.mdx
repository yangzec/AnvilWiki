This page is a ranking framework for Lootbound items and auras. It is not a finished meta list.

<Callout type="warn" title="Ranks are version-sensitive">Aura math and item shapes can shift in a patch. This draft does not assign S-A-B-C letters to named items.</Callout>

## How should I read this Lootbound tier list?

Read it as a checklist, not a verdict. A modest weapon inside the right aura district can beat a stronger piece stranded on the far side of the grid. Rarity is not the rank.

| Letter | Meaning on this wiki |
| --- | --- |
| S | Best-in-slot for a committed district |
| A | Strong and broadly usable |
| B | Fine while progressing |
| C | Situational or starter |

## What makes an item or aura valuable?

Value is shape plus adjacency plus what you must discard. Official store copy: some items have auras that strengthen weapons or armor in adjacent cells, and moving one cell can change the build.

| Criterion | Ask this | Do not ask only this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shape | Can I rotate it without breaking a cluster? | Is the border gold? |
| Aura condition | Does it fire for this weapon or armor class? | Does it have a high listed stat? |
| Neighbors | Which two to four pieces will it actually touch? | Can I dump it in empty space? |
| District | Does it feed damage, defense, or utility? | Can I splash every stat? |
| Replace cost | What aura links die if I take it? | Is it rarer than what I have? |

## What roles do items and auras fill?

The rows below are role buckets from sourced guides, not named legendary items.

| Role bucket | Example job | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Damage aura | Buff adjacent weapons or attack effects | Tooltip condition first |
| Defense aura | Buff adjacent armor or mitigation | Keep the cluster tight |
| Utility / mana aura | Recovery, exhaustion, or resource help | Can extend a run |
| Main weapon | Occupies the damage district | Judge by shape and neighbors |
| Armor / shield | Occupies the defense district | Keep the cluster tight |
| Consumable | Potion or bomb; often no turn cost | Keep on a replaceable edge |
| Order-tagged piece | Amplifies beneficial effects (reported) | Do not mix blindly with Chaos |
| Chaos-tagged piece | Amplifies negative effects on enemies (reported) | Pick one lean per character |

Named item rows will be added only after in-game verification.

## Why can ranks change after a patch?

Because the build is spatial. If an aura's geometry, condition, or adjacent bonus changes, every cluster that used it moves. Re-rank after you read patch notes; do not copy an old letter onto a new build.

Placement rules that feed these ranks are in [inventory auras](/guides/inventory-auras). Opening lanes are in [opening builds](/guides/opening-builds).
