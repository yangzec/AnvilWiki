The final Guardian is Lootbound's end-of-run boss. You do not get one fixed creature every time: you assemble it from the parts you picked at each biome.

<Callout type="info" title="You build this boss">Steam and launch coverage both say each part changes appearance and power. Use that when you pick parts — if you can read the part text.</Callout>

## What is the final Guardian?

It is the boss you face after completing all biomes. Launch coverage reports three biomes, so three part choices feed the fight.

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Official name | Guardian |
| When | After all biomes |
| How it is built | One of two parts at each biome end |

## How do biome part choices change the fight?

Each part changes the Guardian's appearance and power. Players who plan ahead can pick parts that interact with their own build — launch coverage describes this as a deliberate final encounter rather than one identical structure every run.

| Choice point | What you pick | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First biome end | One of two parts | Appearance and power |
| Mid biome end | One of two parts | Appearance and power |
| Later biome end | One of two parts | Appearance and power |
| Final arena | Face the assembled Guardian | The fight you assembled |

## What attacks does the final Guardian use?

No sourced phase list exists.

## What should you bring?

Bring the grid that survived the route, plus leftover consumables. Tips coverage says potions and bombs do not cost a turn, so do not die with a full pouch.
