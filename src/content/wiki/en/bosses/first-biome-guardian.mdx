This page covers the Guardian encounter that closes Lootbound's first biome. The official boss name is not in the allowed sources, so the article stays a draft.

<Callout type="warn" title="Unverified fight data">This draft does not publish HP, attack lists, or drop rates. Only sourced loop facts are listed below.</Callout>

## What is the first biome Guardian?

It is the biome-end encounter that starts the Guardian you will later fight. At the end of each biome you choose one of two boss parts; each part changes the final Guardian's appearance and power.

## Where do you fight this Guardian?

At the end of the first biome, after you travel a branching map from a solo start.

## What attacks does this Guardian use?

Attack patterns are not listed on the Steam page or the allowed guides.

Bring the damage types you already stacked — see [damage types](/guides/damage-types) — and keep armor as the resource that returns after combat.

## What should you bring?

Bring a stable inventory spine and at least one way to deal with armor, because first-biome enemies can wall a single damage language.

| Slot | Bring | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grid core | A placed weapon plus adjacent aura | Placement is the build |
| Armor | A defensive cluster if you have one | Armor restores after combat |
| Consumables | Potions or bombs if you have them | Tips articles say they do not cost a turn |
| Damage mix | One armor-breaker or armor-ignorer if offered | Type names come from tips coverage |

## What does this Guardian drop?

The sourced reward is the part choice itself.

| Reward | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Guardian part (choice of two) | Always offered at biome end. Changes later Guardian appearance and power. |
