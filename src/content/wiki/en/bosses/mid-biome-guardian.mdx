This page covers Lootbound's mid-run biome Guardian. Launch coverage says a run has three biomes.

<Callout type="warn" title="Draft on purpose">Mid-guardian identity is not in the Steam page or the allowed articles. This draft does not invent ranks, HP, or drop rates.</Callout>

## What is the mid-biome Guardian?

It is the biome-end encounter in the middle of a run, where you again choose one of two boss parts. That choice further changes the final Guardian's appearance and power.

## Where do you fight this Guardian?

After you leave the first biome and travel the next branching map.

## What attacks does this Guardian use?

No sourced attack list exists yet. Do not copy patterns from other games.

Keep mana in reserve if the fight can go long. Mana only partly returns between battles; armor returns in full.

## What should you bring?

Bring the district you already committed to — damage, defense, or utility — instead of a last-second rebuild.

| Check | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Grid | Confirm auras still touch the pieces they buff |
| Types | Keep more than one damage language if you can |
| Resources | Do not enter on empty mana |

## What does this Guardian drop?

The sourced reward is the part choice itself.

| Reward | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Guardian part (choice of two) | Offered at biome end. Stacks with earlier part choices. |
