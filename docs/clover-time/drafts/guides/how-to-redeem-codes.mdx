## Where is the actual redemption field?

Beebom reports a bottom-left Codes option. This is a third-party claim awaiting gameplay verification, not a confirmed step for players to follow.

## What conditions must a player meet?

Beebom reports joining the AnimeCore Studio group, completing the tutorial, and restarting. Test each condition independently rather than combining it with another site's route.

## What do failed redemption messages mean?

Capture the exact message. Distinguish an already-used code, an eligibility restriction, a typo, and confirmed expiry. Do not infer expiry from a generic failure.
