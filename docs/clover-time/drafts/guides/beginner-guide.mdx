## What does the official game description confirm?

The official Roblox description lists Grimoire progression, action combat, spell combos, awakenings, Magic Broom flight, Magic Squads, and AFK World rewards. It does not establish their unlock order.

## What should a new player do first?

Check the tutorial and quest prompts before spending resources. The reported routes below come from two articles on the same independent player wiki. This draft's author has read the articles but has not tested the routes or watched their underlying recordings.

## How are starter tools reportedly obtained?

According to the [player wiki's tool walkthrough](https://clovertime.wiki/gear/clover-time-how-to-get-tools), Lily stands outside the starting church and gives a free Rake when you accept her potato quest. The article says the Rake arrives equipped and that harvesting uses E beside a fully grown crop. Those details still need an independent interface check.

The same article reports that a lumberjack's firewood quest gives a free Axe in your inventory. It does not establish the lumberjack's exact name, a complete route from spawn, or the controls for equipping the Axe. This draft therefore cannot yet provide a complete tool-acquisition walkthrough.

The article connects potatoes with early quests and crafting, and wood with crafting. Specific recipes and quantities are outside the verified scope of this draft.

## What is the reported first Grimoire route?

The [player wiki's Grimoire walkthrough](https://clovertime.wiki/magic/clover-time-how-to-get-grimoire) describes this opening sequence:

1. Open the starter Normal Chest, then its Medium Chest, to obtain a Mana Crystal.
2. Follow the compass tower icon to the Grimoire Tower.
3. Find the rolling point inside and check its current cost before accepting a roll.
4. After the animation, check your hand and inventory for the resulting Grimoire.

This is a third-party reported sequence, not a tested set of instructions. The article reports one Mana Crystal per roll and distinguishes that resource from Spins used for character rerolls. Read the current game prompt rather than assuming the reported cost remains unchanged.

The source also describes different tutorial states: some players receive a tower key from a nearby NPC, while others enter directly. The NPC's name and the conditions for those routes are unresolved. Manual equipment controls and the input for using a first spell are also missing, so this route does not yet meet the full beginner-guide acceptance criteria.

## Which resources should a beginner save?

Identify resources from the game interface and verify their uses before spending. Do not infer a crafting recipe, roll probability, or storage rule from another Roblox game. This draft makes no recommendation to reroll an existing character configuration or repeatedly spend Mana Crystals.
